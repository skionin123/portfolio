My story

I’ve built a career by learning what the work needs next.

I’m Niño Anthony Vitualla, a multidisciplinary digital professional with hands-on experience in email marketing, cold outreach, lead generation, virtual assistance, social media, graphic design, e-commerce, web support, and AI-assisted development.

I’m also the CEO of MailAtlas and Paddle Orbit. Leading my own products has strengthened the way I approach client work: I think about outcomes, users, priorities, quality, and how a task connects to the bigger business goal.

Before moving deeper into digital work, I worked as a Loan Officer in 2018. That experience built my foundation in client communication, documentation, follow-up, account monitoring and responsibility around sensitive business processes.

Today, I’m most valuable to teams that need someone adaptable — a person who can research prospects in the morning, improve an outreach campaign after lunch, update a website, create a visual asset, test an AI workflow, and still keep the work organized.
